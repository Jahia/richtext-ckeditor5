Added the Jahia GenAI connector as a provider of the AI Assistant, so the assistant is no longer limited to OpenAI. To use it, set aiType=genai in the CKEditor 5 configuration and configure the GenAI connector. The AI Assistant answer now appears while it is written, and the assistant also works on a Jahia installed under a context path.
